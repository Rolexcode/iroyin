# Custom incident corpus design · v1.0

Record 24 acted clips: eight per scenario, twelve per language pair, twelve quiet and twelve everyday-noise. Use at least four adult speakers with a mix of genders, ages, accents, speaking rates, and code-switch patterns. Each speaker reviews the fictional script premise, improvises naturally, and signs the corpus consent record. Never use a real unresolved complaint or real personal data.

Each prompt below is a **fact brief**, not a line to read verbatim. The actor may code-switch naturally but must preserve every listed reference slot. The annotator creates a verbatim transcript afterward.
